ILOCOS NORTE COLLEGE OF ARTS AND TRADES

Practicing and observing technical learning

My Field Study at INCAT gave me opportunities to observe how technical learning activities are prepared and delivered. In classrooms and computer-related learning activities, I paid attention to the instructions teachers give, the way learners respond, and the importance of organizing practical tasks carefully.

I participated in classroom assistance, observed teaching strategies, and supported activities involving practical computer work and structured document preparation. These tasks made the relationship between clear explanations and learner practice more apparent to me.

Areas of learning

  • Classroom routines and strategies. I examined how questioning, explanations, and management practices create an organized learning environment.
  • Hands-on technical learning. Activities involving computer systems highlighted the value of guided practice, safe procedures, and step-by-step instructions.
  • Assessment and teaching preparation. Quizzes, performance rubrics, classroom tasks, and demonstration plans showed how evidence of learning can inform instruction.
PERSONAL REFLECTION

This experience helped me appreciate that teaching a technical skill requires planning for both understanding and actual performance. Good instruction makes complex tasks clearer while giving students room to practice.

02IN-CAMPUS

MMSU LABORATORY HIGH SCHOOL

Understanding planning, assessment, and learner needs

At MMSU Laboratory High School, I observed teaching practices and classroom processes that broadened my perspective on educational preparation. The activities documented in my portfolio include learner-diversity observation, classroom management, assessment, records and school forms, technology integration, and lesson planning.

Preparing instructional materials related to construction site preparation allowed me to think about how learning goals, explanations, activities, and assessments can be aligned. I also learned to consider how the environment and the characteristics of learners influence the choices teachers make.

Areas of learning

  • Learner differences. Observation activities encouraged me to consider readiness, confidence, participation, and forms of classroom support.
  • Instructional alignment. Lesson reconstruction and preparation connected objectives with teaching procedures and assessment methods.
  • Classroom documentation. Learning about records and school forms highlighted the administrative responsibilities teachers carry alongside instruction.
PERSONAL REFLECTION

This placement reinforced the importance of preparation and adaptability. Planning a lesson is essential, but teachers must also observe how learners engage and adjust their support appropriately.
