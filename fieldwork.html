PLACEMENT / 01
IN
OFF-CAMPUS
ILOCOS NORTE COLLEGE OF ARTS AND TRADES

Learning through hands-on technical activities

At the Ilocos Norte College of Arts and Trades (INCAT), I experienced a classroom environment where technical subjects involve both explanation and direct practice. My Field Study work included observing instructional approaches, assisting learners, and participating in classroom activities involving computer systems and document preparation.

These experiences helped me appreciate why technical lessons need clear demonstrations, safe working procedures, appropriate learning resources, and opportunities for students to apply their knowledge. They also encouraged me to reflect on how a teacher can provide guidance without taking away the learner's opportunity to practice independently.

01

Observing learning routines

Paying attention to classroom management, teaching strategies, questioning techniques, and how learning activities are organized.

02

Supporting practical activities

Taking part in learner assistance during computer hardware tasks, document-formatting activities, and classroom exercises.

03

Preparing and assessing learning

Connecting lesson preparation, performance tasks, quizzes, and classroom observations to the intended learning objectives.

PLACEMENT / 02
LH
IN-CAMPUS
MMSU LABORATORY HIGH SCHOOL

Understanding classroom planning and assessment

At MMSU Laboratory High School, I observed classroom procedures, assessment practices, and the preparation of learning materials. My Field Study documents also cover lesson reconstruction, classroom records, learner diversity, and teaching strategies, giving me opportunities to examine different elements of the teaching-learning process.

During lesson preparation related to construction site preparation, I considered the importance of organizing content into clear objectives, appropriate activities, and assessments. Observing classroom practice helped me understand that a well-prepared lesson also requires flexibility and awareness of the learners and their setting.

01

Understanding learners

Considering learner readiness, classroom participation, and the different ways students may need support.

02

Examining assessment

Observing how questions, tasks, and learning records may be used to check understanding and document progress.

03

Developing lesson materials

Preparing and reviewing lesson components to connect objectives, teaching strategies, activities, and evidence of learning.

REFLECTION / WHAT I LEARNED

The value of observing before teaching

Both placements emphasized that effective teaching involves more than subject knowledge. Classroom routines, meaningful participation, relevant materials, and responsive assessment all contribute to the learning experience. Each observation and classroom task provided another opportunity to improve my readiness for future teaching responsibilities.
