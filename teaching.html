01 / DETAILED LESSON PLAN▣

System Unit Assembly & Disassembly

This lesson plan focuses on identifying computer components and following safe, organized procedures for assembling and disassembling a system unit. The topic is suited to hands-on learning because students need to understand the purpose of components as well as the sequence of practical tasks.

Its instructional emphasis includes preparation, safety considerations, step-by-step procedures, and opportunities to demonstrate understanding through practical activities.

02 / FINAL DEMONSTRATION PLAN▤

Creating and Formatting a Structured Document

This lesson plan centers on preparing a properly organized document using Microsoft Word. Students are introduced to the functions and formatting features that support readability, consistency, and professional presentation of written work.

The learning focus includes applying document-formatting tools, following guided instructions, and checking whether the finished work meets the expectations of the activity.

03 / PRESENTATION MATERIAL▥

Final Demonstration Presentation

The final demonstration presentation serves as an instructional aid for the structured-document lesson. It helps organize the sequence of content, activities, and explanations so that learners can follow the topic and connect concepts with the work they are expected to complete.

As a teaching resource, the presentation complements the lesson plan by providing a visual structure for delivery, classroom interaction, and the discussion of document-formatting tasks.

FROM PLANNING TO PRACTICE

Preparing meaningful instruction

These Field Study activities strengthened my appreciation for organizing lessons around clear learning goals while considering students' readiness, available resources, and appropriate forms of assessment.

01

Set a clear objective

Identify the knowledge or skill that learners are expected to develop by the end of the lesson.

02

Choose a relevant activity

Connect explanations and demonstrations with tasks that make the lesson practical and understandable.

03

Check understanding

Consider questions, outputs, or performance tasks that show whether students have met the intended goals.
